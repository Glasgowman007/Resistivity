How length, area and temperature affect resistance

How to use it

  1. Pick the conductor material and set the cable length, cross-sectional area and temperature.
  2. Work out the resistance yourself using R = ρL ÷ A.
  3. Check your answer below and open the working to follow each step. Try doubling the length or the area and see what happens.
